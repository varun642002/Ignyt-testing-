IGNYT Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 July 2026 · Last updated: 1 August 2026

Welcome to IGNYT ("we", "our", or "us"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how IGNYT collects, uses, stores, protects, and processes your information when you use the IGNYT mobile application and related services. By using IGNYT, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. About IGNYT

IGNYT is a fitness and wellness application designed to help users:

  • Track workouts
  • Log nutrition and calories
  • Monitor body weight
  • Record hydration
  • Track fasting
  • Track supplements
  • View fitness progress
  • Connect with Google Health Connect
  • Synchronize health information (with permission)

IGNYT is intended for personal fitness tracking only and is not a medical device.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, IGNYT may collect the following information.

Account information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number — optional. You may add one to your profile during setup or later in Personal Information. It is stored on your device with the rest of your profile and is not used to sign you in, verify you, or contact you.
  • Profile photo — optional. If you add one, it is resized and stored on your device with your profile. It is not uploaded anywhere unless you turn on cloud sync.
  • Firebase User ID

Profile information

You may choose to provide:

  • Age
  • Gender
  • Height
  • Weight
  • Fitness goals
  • Activity level

Providing this information is optional unless required for specific features.

Workout information

IGNYT allows you to store exercises, sets, repetitions, weight lifted, workout duration, rest time, workout history, and notes.

Nutrition information

You may log meals, calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, fiber, water intake, vitamins, and supplements.

Body measurements

You may record weight, body fat, BMI, waist, chest, arms, hips, legs, and progress photos (if enabled).

3. Health Connect Data

IGNYT may request permission to read and/or write selected health and fitness data through Google Health Connect. Depending on the permissions you grant, IGNYT may access:

  • Weight
  • Calories burned
  • Exercise sessions
  • Steps
  • Distance
  • Active energy
  • Hydration
  • Sleep (if supported)
  • Heart rate (if supported)

IGNYT only accesses the specific data types that are required for the features you choose to use. Health Connect permissions are granted and managed entirely by you.

4. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Create your account
  • Authenticate your identity
  • Save workouts
  • Display fitness statistics
  • Track progress
  • Calculate nutrition
  • Display charts
  • Sync Health Connect data
  • Restore backups
  • Improve app performance
  • Provide customer support

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Health Data

Health and fitness information is considered sensitive. IGNYT uses Health Connect data only to provide the health and fitness features requested by you. We do not use your health information for:

  • Advertising
  • Marketing
  • User profiling
  • Selling to third parties

Health data is processed only for the fitness features available inside the application.

6. Google Sign-In

If you sign in with Google, we may receive your name, email address, and profile picture. We do not receive your Google password. Authentication is handled securely by Google and Firebase.

7. Firebase Authentication

IGNYT uses Firebase Authentication for secure login. Email and password is the only sign-in method. Firebase securely manages authentication credentials.

Phone number authentication is not used. A phone number you add to your profile plays no part in signing in.

8. Payments

IGNYT does not currently take payments. There is no subscription, no in-app purchase and no billing integration in the app — nothing in it can charge you.

If paid features are added in future, they would be processed through Google Play Billing, and this policy will be updated before that happens. Under that arrangement IGNYT would still never receive or store:

  • Credit card numbers
  • Debit card numbers
  • UPI credentials
  • Banking information

Payment processing would be handled entirely by Google Play.

9. Analytics and crash reporting

IGNYT collects no analytics and no crash reports. The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK and no performance-monitoring SDK. Nothing about your app version, device model, usage or crashes is sent anywhere.

This is a deliberate choice rather than an oversight, and it is why nothing in this policy describes an analytics provider: there isn't one.

10. Data Storage

Your data may be stored on your device and in secure Firebase services (where applicable). We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information.

11. Data Sharing

IGNYT does not sell or rent your personal information. Information may only be shared:

  • With Google Firebase for authentication and app functionality
  • When required by law
  • To protect legal rights or prevent fraud

12. Data Security

We use industry-standard security practices including HTTPS encryption, secure authentication, Firebase security, access controls, and secure cloud infrastructure. While we strive to protect your information, no system can guarantee absolute security.

13. Your Rights

You may:

  • Update your profile
  • Change your password
  • Delete workouts
  • Delete nutrition logs
  • Delete body measurements
  • Disconnect Health Connect
  • Revoke Google permissions
  • Request account deletion

14. Account Deletion

You may delete your account at any time. When an account is deleted:

  • Personal profile information is removed or anonymized, subject to applicable legal or operational requirements.
  • Stored fitness data associated with the account is deleted according to our retention practices.
  • Health Connect permissions can be revoked from your device settings, and Health Connect data remains under your control.

15. Children's Privacy

IGNYT is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

16. Third-Party Services

IGNYT may use services including:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Google Health Connect
  • Google Sign-In

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy.

17. International Users

If you use IGNYT outside India, your information may be processed in countries where our service providers operate, subject to applicable legal safeguards.

18. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The updated version will display a new "Last Updated" date. Continued use of IGNYT after changes means you accept the revised policy.
