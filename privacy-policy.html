Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 27, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. IGNYT is committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about how your data is collected, stored, and used.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use IGNYT, we may collect the following information:

  • Workout plans and workout history
  • Exercise logs
  • Nutrition and calorie tracking information
  • Body measurements and fitness progress
  • Goals and training preferences
  • User account information (if you choose to sign in)
  • Cloud backup information (only if enabled by you)
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information (if enabled)

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide workout tracking
  • Save your exercise history
  • Generate progress charts
  • Improve application performance
  • Restore your data from backups
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve future versions of IGNYT

3. Google Services

IGNYT may use Google services including Firebase Authentication, Firebase Cloud Services, Google Drive Backup, and future Google APIs when you choose to enable them. These services are governed by Google's own privacy policies.

4. Health and Fitness Data

IGNYT stores health and fitness information only to provide features such as workout tracking, nutrition logging, progress analytics, and performance insights. Health information is never sold. Future integrations such as Google Health Connect will only access data after you explicitly grant permission.

5. Data Sharing

IGNYT does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share information when required to provide services that you choose to use.

6. Data Storage

  • Most data is stored locally on your device.
  • Cloud synchronization is optional.
  • You control whether backups are created.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to help protect your information. However, no method of storage or internet transmission is completely secure.

8. Your Rights

You may:

  • Delete your workout history
  • Delete nutrition logs
  • Disable cloud backup
  • Remove your account (when available)
  • Request deletion of synchronized data

9. Third-Party Services

IGNYT may integrate with services such as:

  • Firebase
  • Google Drive
  • Google Health Connect (future)
  • Notion (optional future integration)

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy.

10. Children's Privacy

IGNYT is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. The latest version will always be available on this page.
